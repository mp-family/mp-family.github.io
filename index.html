Our story

One couple. Seven branches. Many lives.

Every family begins with two people. Ours began with Mudalappa and Puttamma. Their sons and daughters started families of their own, and today the MP family spans four generations.

This page celebrates every one of us: the elders who built the foundation, the parents who kept it strong, and the children carrying it forward.
